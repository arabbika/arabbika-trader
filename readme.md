# 🛍️ Arabbika Trader

**An AI-powered pricing engine and WhatsApp message generator for informal traders in emerging markets.**

[Live Demo](https://arabbika-trader.onrender.com/)

## 📖 Overview

Informal traders and micro-merchants (selling on WhatsApp, Facebook Marketplace, or at the taxi rank) often sell at a loss because they forget to factor in transport, data, and shrinkage (broken/lost stock). 

Arabbika Trader calculates your **true cost**, tells you exactly what to charge to hit your profit margin, and uses AI to generate a professional, copy-paste WhatsApp message for your products.

## ✨ Features

*   **True Cost Calculator:** Factors in wholesale cost, transport, data, and shrinkage.
*   **Profit Leak Warning:** Alerts you if your current selling price is losing you money.
*   **AI WhatsApp Flyer:** Generates a clean, professional sales message for your WhatsApp Status.
*   **Saved Products:** Keeps a list of your inventory so you don't have to re-enter data.
*   **Sales Tracker:** Tap "+1" to track how many items you've sold and your total profit.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

*   **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, Vanilla JS
*   **Backend:** Node.js, Express
*   **AI Engine:** xAI (Grok)
*   **Hosting:** Render
